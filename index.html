How Does the Age Calculator Work?

Enter your date of birth and click the "Calculate My Age" button. The calculator uses today's date to calculate your exact age in years, months and days.

What Can This Age Calculator Calculate?

This free age calculator can calculate your exact age, total months, total weeks, total days, day of birth, next birthday and the number of days remaining until your next birthday.

Why Use an Online Age Calculator?

Calculating age manually can sometimes be confusing, especially when months have different numbers of days. This calculator provides a quick and simple way to calculate your age using your date of birth.
